# Key Terms

| Term | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| Black hat | A person who uses hacking skills for illegal or harmful purposes. |
| Ransomware | Malware that locks or encrypts files and demands payment. |
| Phishing | A fake message designed to trick someone into clicking, opening a file, or sharing information. |
| Insider threat | Risk caused by someone inside an organisation who misuses access, intentionally or accidentally. |
| Hacktivist | A person or group using cyber activity to promote a political or social cause. |
| Vulnerability | A weakness in a system, application, process, or human practice that may be exploited. |
| Mitigation | An action that reduces the chance or impact of a security incident. |
| MFA | Multi-factor authentication; two or more different checks to confirm identity. |
